# KICKOFF — TopTechHR: the HR edition of Key Personnel

You are building **TopTechHR**, a static web app for an HR professional at a small technical firm whose hires must satisfy a contract or a customer. It answers one question in four steps — what is going on with this opening — and returns a one-page plan that is ranked by fit, graded by evidence and cited line by line, with a notebook that turns highlighted passages into quotes and exports a rich-text action plan. **The wordmark is "TopTechHR"** and the logo is in `brand/`. You are the build-and-delivery role; the content is not yours to write.

**Content: one pack, two apps.** TopTechHR carries **no evidence of its own**. Every fact, figure, rule and citation comes from the **Key Personnel content pack**, consumed as a pinned dependency (§2). TopTechHR adds only a thin **HR layer** (`hr-layer/`: plain names, the Start flow, the moments HR meets rules at, the action-plan template, glossary aliases), and `tools/validate_hr_layer.py` fails the build if any HR-layer line points at a record that does not exist or carries a number or a citation of its own. Key Personnel stays live as the evidence edition; TopTechHR links to it as "the full evidence".

This is a single-shot build: work through every section below, in order, without stopping for confirmation, and finish with the §6b ship step and the scrum note.

## 1. Hard rules

All of Key Personnel's hard rules apply unchanged (`../key-personnel/KICKOFF.md` §1): never invent content; body text rendered as written; uncited prose fails the build; synthesis markers stay visible; nothing implies peer review, official status or advice; **nothing is ever recommended** (the Plan ranks by the pathfinder's match count and says so in one line; actions are listed with their evidence grade and their limits line, never ordered by any merit score); effect sizes exactly as the record carries them with the population beside them and `what_it_does_not_do` beneath; the ledger is a ledger; compilation-sourced is shown as such; pending is pending, superseded is struck; where sources disagree show both; no runtime fetches of pack content; fail loudly. Plus, for this app:

- **The HR layer renames and arranges; it never adds a fact.** A plain name from `actions.yaml` or `rules.yaml` renders **above** the pack's own name, never instead of it; the pack text follows unchanged beneath. If a plain name is missing, the pack name alone is shown. Nothing in `hr-layer/` is ever cited, and the build fails if a line there carries a `[n]`, a percentage or a dollar figure (the validator checks this).
- **No firm, no account, no server, no analytics.** No firm is named anywhere, including in examples. Everything personal (Start answers, notebooks, the chosen state) lives in `localStorage` with a visible "stays in this browser" note; nothing leaves the browser except the build-time BLS refresh, stated where it is shown. No cookie banner is needed because there are no cookies.
- **Alabama and the defense industry are the default lens, not the only one.** The state selector defaults to `us-al` and every state record is selectable; an Alabama or Huntsville figure is **never shown without its national counterpart** when the record carries one (`counterpart`); the Defense tab is one of three, not the first.
- **Every rule shows its date and its text.** A gate or overlay renders with `governing_text`, `as_of`, its `pending_changes`, and the compilation flag when `primary_pending` is true; the "Official text pending (read on a mirror)" wording applies to the Alabama overlays whose sources are mirror-read (the record's `text` says so).
- **The pack version is visible.** The top band on every page reads "Evidence as of <pack manifest.as_of> · pack <tag>", where `<tag>` is the pinned pack tag (§2). `/about` lists the pack tag, the pack's `README.md` sweep summary, and the link to Key Personnel.
- **Plain, descriptive voice in the product's own strings** (labels, the four questions, the template, empty states, error states): no imperatives aimed at the reader's situation and no second person beyond the UI's own affordances ("Your plan", "Add to plan", "Saved in this browser" are affordances; "You should post a range" is advice and is forbidden). The product strings are listed in one file (`src/strings.ts`) so they can be reviewed in one place.
- **Delivery mode is `pages`.** The app ends up public — §6b. **GitHub identity:** `danieladamek`; verify with `gh auth status` before any push. **The repo does not exist yet**: create it in §6b.
- Vault contract: don't touch anything outside this folder except the K-item's `state` in `../../notes/Backlog/` (and the pack repo tag in §2, which lives in `../key-personnel/`); leave a scrum note at `notes/Scrums/YYMMDD.md` in the exact format from `../../../CLAUDE.md` with `app: toptechhr`.

### 4b. Plan (`/plan`)
One page, reading order, print-ready, built from the Start answers:
1. Title "<pattern label>, at the <stage> stage" with the condition chips, the state chip, "Change answers", **Print**, **Add all to plan**.
2. One line: "Ranked by how many of your answers each item matches. Nothing here is recommended over anything else."
3. **Check these first**: the matched constraints as cards (score as "Matches N of your answers"; the pack name; the first two `how_to_tell` lines; "Why it matched · sources" expands the reasons in the question labels' words and the sources). Constraints with score 0 are hidden; "N more matched fewer answers" expands the rest.
4. **Actions, with their evidence**: every lever of every matched constraint, grouped by constraint in `plan-order.json` order, each card carrying the plain name above the pack name, `evidence.grade` as a chip, `evidence.effect` + `evidence.population` + `evidence.k_n` as "Effect as reported", **`what_it_does_not_do` as "Does not:"**, "Acts on: <constraints>", the "Do first" line where a precondition edge exists, a `sector: defense` chip where an enabler on the lever carries it, and **Add to plan**. "Tools and programs for this plan" lists the matched levers' enablers (plain name, `what`, `cost`, `conditions`, `geo`/`sector` chips, official link).
5. **Rules to check**: the gates the matched constraints and levers touch (`gates[]`), split Federal / <State>, each with an amber "Check" tag, the plain name, one line from `applies_when`, `as_of`, and the state's overlays that modify them (status chip; the compilation tag; the "Official text pending" wording where the overlay says so). The state's `state_gates` appear under the state heading with the "Statute" chip.
6. **Numbers that frame it**: the indicators the matched records and stages reference (`indicators[]` on stages; `metrics[].benchmark[]`), Alabama/Huntsville ones with their `counterpart` in the same card, the refreshed BLS value beside the pack value where present; four to eight cards, never more.
7. **Sources on this page**: every reference cited by anything above, by number, each a fold-out card as in Key Personnel.
A Plan with no answers shows the Start steps inline instead. Everything on the Plan carries an anchor so the Notebook can point back at it.

### 4c. The Notebook (replaces the notepad)
A docked right panel (a drawer at ≤ 900 px) on every page, toggled from the header; `/notebook` lists notebooks; `/notebook/:id` is the full-page editor; `/notebook/:id/print` is the print view.
- **Model:** `{id, title, created, updated, startAnswers, doc (TipTap JSON), quotes: [{id, text, anchor: {route, recordType, recordId, sectionId?}, title, citations: [n], refs: [{n, citation}], asOf?, capturedAt}], actions: [{leverId, owner, by, measure}], rules: [gateId | stateOverlayKey], numbers: [indicatorId]}` in `localStorage` under one key with a schema version; several notebooks; "New notebook" opens on the **action-plan template** from `hr-layer/plan-template.yaml` (six sections as headings with placeholder text; the Sources section is generated at export and read-only in the editor).
- **Quote from anything:** on `mouseup`/`selectionchange` with a non-empty selection inside pack-rendered content (review sections, record fields, primers, glossary definitions, indicator cards, overlay text), show a small floating control ("Quote" and, on a lever card, "Add action"). Quote inserts a `quoteBlock` node (a custom TipTap node) carrying the selected text and its provenance resolved from the nearest anchor: the record or section title, the `[n]` tokens inside the selection (or, if none, the citations of the enclosing block), each reference's `citation` line, the route, and the record's `as_of` where it has one. The node renders as a block quote with a one-line source beneath and a link back. Provenance is **never** separable from the quote: copy, export and print all carry it.
- **Add to plan / Add action / Add rule / Add number** fill the matching template section: an action row carries the plain name, the pack name, "Does not: …" verbatim, and editable Owner / By / Measure fields rendered as `[owner]`, `[date]`, `[measure]` placeholders until filled; a rule row carries the name, `governing_text`, `as_of` and the compilation flag; a number row carries value, counterpart, period, publisher and series.
- **Editor:** headings, bold, italic, bulleted and numbered lists, task lists, links, block quotes, undo/redo, keyboard shortcuts, a placeholder; no images in v1.
- **Export:** `.docx` (via `docx`: title, the six sections, quotes as indented paragraphs with the source line in a smaller run, actions as a table, a Sources section numbered in pack order), **PDF** (the print route with a print stylesheet; the Export menu opens it and calls `window.print()`), **HTML** (self-contained, the quote provenance as `<figcaption>`), **Markdown** (`turndown`; quotes as `>` blocks with the source line), and **JSON** (the model; "Import" reads it back, merging by id). The export filename is `<title>-<date>`.
- **Persistence line** in the panel footer: "Saved in this browser only. Export to keep it. Nothing is sent anywhere." The dev-only file-autosave endpoint from the explorer apps is **not** carried.
